About the Center

We aim to transform pediatric physical therapy into an engaging and enjoyable experience using VR, motion sensors, and robotic support. Our goal is to improve therapy adherence, reduce treatment duration, and make rehabilitation fun and accessible.

Main Features

  • Immersive VR Therapy Games
  • Robotic Sports Assistants
  • Smart Motion Sensors for real-time feedback
  • Adjustable Supporting Suit
  • AI-based Adaptive System
  • Remote Monitoring Dashboard
